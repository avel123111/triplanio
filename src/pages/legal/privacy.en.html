Overview

We collect what the Service needs to plan your trip, and nothing we cannot explain. We do not sell personal data. Analytics only starts after you agree to it, and you can delete your account at any time.

This Policy explains what we collect, why, on what legal basis, who else sees it and what you can do about it. It applies to the Triplanio website, web application and connected assistants.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Triplanio OÜ, Harju maakond, Tallinn, Estonia. For any question about your data, write to privacy@triplanio.com.

What we collect

Account data
email address, name, chosen language, avatar if you upload one
Trip data
cities, dates, bookings, transfers, notes, budget entries and files you add to a Trip
Participant data
the email addresses you use to invite people, and the status of those invitations
Payment data
subscription status, plan, period and the provider’s identifiers — never full card numbers
Technical data
IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, error reports
Usage data
which screens are opened and which actions are taken — only after you consent to analytics

How we use it

  • to create and maintain your Account and show you your Trips;
  • to share a Trip with the Participants you invited;
  • to send transactional messages — invitations, reminders, password resets, receipts;
  • to process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • to detect abuse, keep the Service secure and fix errors;
  • to understand how the Service is used and improve it, where you have consented.

We do not use your Trip content for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Legal bases

Contract
everything needed to give you the Service you signed up for
Consent
analytics, product emails and any optional cookie — withdrawable at any time
Legitimate interests
security, abuse prevention and basic service diagnostics
Legal obligation
accounting and tax records for payments

Cookies and analytics

Strictly necessary cookies keep you signed in and remember your language. They cannot be switched off, because without them the Service does not work.

Analytics loads only after you agree in the consent banner. Until you agree, no analytics code is downloaded and no events are sent. You can change your choice at any time from the footer of the site.

Who else sees your data

We share data only with the processors that make the Service work, and only to the extent each of them needs:

Hosting and database
stores your Account and Trip data
Payment provider
processes payments and holds the card details we never see
Email delivery
sends transactional messages
Maps and geocoding
turns place names into coordinates to draw your route
Error monitoring
receives technical error reports with personal details stripped out
Analytics
receives usage events, if you consented

Participants of a Trip see the Trip content you shared with them. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Our infrastructure is in the European Union. Where a processor operates outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

While your account exists
Account and Trip data
30 days after deletion
backups, after which they are overwritten
7 years
payment and accounting records, as tax law requires
90 days
technical logs and error reports

When you delete your Account we erase or anonymise your personal details. Content you added to a shared Trip may remain visible to the other Participants without your name attached.

Your rights

  • access a copy of your data;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete your account and data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • take your data elsewhere in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, without affecting what happened before.

Write to privacy@triplanio.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country; in Estonia this is the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit, data is encrypted at rest, and access to production systems is limited to the people who need it and logged. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never visible to us.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the supervisory authority within the time the law allows.

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to privacy@triplanio.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this Policy

We update this Policy when the Service or the law changes. The current version is always on this page. We announce material changes at least 14 days before they take effect.

Contact

Data protection questions: privacy@triplanio.com. Postal address: Triplanio OÜ, Harju maakond, Tallinn, Estonia.
