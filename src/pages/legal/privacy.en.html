Overview

We collect what the Service needs to plan your trips, and nothing we cannot explain. We do not sell personal data, and you can delete your account at any time.

This Policy explains what we collect, why, who else is involved, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. It applies to the Triplanio website, web application and connected assistants (together, the “Service”).

Who is responsible

The data controller is a self-employed entrepreneur (sole trader) registered in Spain, trading as Triplanio. Business details are available on request. For any question about your data, write to info@triplanio.com.

Analytics and advertising — what actually happens

We want this to be precise, because it is often written vaguely:

Before you answer the cookie banner (and if you choose “Necessary only”), our product analytics (PostHog) collects nothing at all: no events are sent and nothing is stored on your device. Error monitoring (Sentry) and privacy-friendly aggregate visit counting (Vercel Web Analytics) run without storing anything on your device.

If you choose “Accept all”, analytics starts and stores identifiers on your device (see the next section), so we can recognise the same browser across days. With the same choice, we load advertising measurement from Google Ads and OpenAI Ads (ChatGPT Ads), which store advertising identifiers on your device to attribute sign-ups to ad clicks. To improve conversion matching we send Google and OpenAI a SHA-256 hash of your email address — never the raw address.

Interface playback. With the same choice, some sessions are also kept as a playback (also called a session replay) of how the interface behaved as you moved through it, which we use to find where the product is confusing or broken. All text and all input fields are masked, and profile photos are blocked, before anything leaves your browser: we see layout, clicks and navigation, never what you or anyone else wrote. Nothing is recorded before you accept, only sessions matching rules we configure are kept (for example, a session in which a purchase was started), and replays are deleted after 30 days.

You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer or in your account. Withdrawing consent stops analytics storage and advertising measurement; identifiers already stored are removed, and any remaining Google cookies can be deleted in your browser settings.

Once you have accepted and are signed in, what you do in the product is recorded under your account number (never your name or email) — this is how we understand how the product is used, and it is based on our legitimate interest. It stores nothing on your device unless you accepted analytics cookies; you can object at any time via support@triplanio.com.

Independent of the banner, when a new account is created we record the campaign tags of the visit that led to the sign-up (for example utm_source or an ad-click ID) as part of the account record — this is a one-time note about how you found us, not ongoing tracking.

Cookies and data stored on your device

Strictly necessary

Preferences
theme, language, units, list view settings, and unsaved drafts of a trip you are creating (kept only for the current tab)
Analytics — only after you accept

Advertising — only after you accept

Third-party components may use their own storage when they load: Mapbox (maps) stores technical identifiers, and Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment pages.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the Service, each limited to its role:

Supabase
our database, authentication and file storage. Hosted in the EU (Ireland)
Vercel
hosts and delivers the website (EU region) and provides cookieless aggregate visit statistics
Railway
hosts our automation server (EU, Netherlands), which delivers invitation emails and notifications and runs the AI features
Stripe
processes payments. Receives your email address and account ID; card details stay with Stripe
PostHog (EU)
product analytics and masked interface playbacks, as described above
Sentry (EU)
error monitoring. Reports are scrubbed of personal details; session replays are captured only when an error occurs and mask all text and inputs
Google
three separate roles: sign-in with Google (if you use it), advertising measurement (only with your consent), and a small icon service that shows website icons next to booking links you add
OpenAI
advertising measurement for ChatGPT Ads (only with your consent), used to attribute sign-ups to ad clicks
Mapbox
maps and routes. When you open a map, your browser talks to Mapbox and sends the coordinates of the area and route being displayed
LocationIQ
address search. Receives the address text being searched (not your account identity). Based in the US
AI model providers
when you use AI features, the relevant content is processed by third-party AI models: booking recognition uses Mistral (OCR and text extraction), and the Telegram assistant uses Google Gemini. See the AI section below
Telegram
only if you connect the Telegram assistant: we store the chat ID and Telegram name of the connected chat and send trip reminders and replies to it
Booking partners
when we show partner offers (Stay22, Viator and others), availability requests include the destination and dates of your trip, but not your identity. Some partner images load directly from the partners’ servers. If you click a partner link, you leave the Service and that partner’s own terms and privacy policy apply; we record the click (which partner and for which trip) to account for affiliate commissions

Participants of a trip see the trip content you shared with them. We also disclose data where the law requires it.

AI features

AI features run only when you invoke them, and only the relevant content is processed:

  • Booking recognition — the file or text you submit is processed to extract the booking details.
  • AI trip planning — your planning request and the conversation with the planner.
  • Trip assistant (chat and Telegram) — your question and recent messages of that trip chat, so the assistant has context.

This content is processed by the AI providers named above to generate the answer. We do not use your content to train AI models. Usage records (which feature, when, and how much computation it used) are kept for cost accounting — they do not include the text of your requests.

International transfers

Your data is stored in the European Union: the database and files in Ireland, our automation server in the Netherlands. Some of our providers are US companies or process limited data in the United States (for example Stripe, Google, LocationIQ). Those transfers are covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses.

Sharing within trips and public links

Trip participants see the content of that trip according to their role. Documents you mark as private are visible only to you.

If you create a public link to a trip, anyone who has the link can view the trip — route, bookings, activities, notes and participant names (files and personal documents are not included). The link stays valid until the trip is deleted. Share it thoughtfully.

Your rights

  • access a copy of your data;
  • correct anything inaccurate — most account details can be edited in the app;
  • delete your account and data — directly in the account settings. Deletion erases or anonymises your personal details (email, name, avatar), deletes your private documents, notifications and personal integrations, and signs you out everywhere. An active subscription must be cancelled first. Shared trip content and legally required payment records remain, without your identity attached;
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time — for analytics and advertising via “Cookie settings” — without affecting what happened before.

Write to info@triplanio.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es), or the authority of the country where you live.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit and data is encrypted at rest. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never visible to us. Access to production systems is limited and authenticated. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the supervisory authority within the time the law requires.

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to info@triplanio.com and we will delete it.
