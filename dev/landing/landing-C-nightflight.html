09FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Triplanio free to use?+
Yes — start planning for free with all the core features: trips, bookings, budget, documents and the timeline. Advanced collaboration and AI features are part of the Pro plan.
How does the AI booking recognition work?+
Forward a confirmation email, paste text or upload a PDF or screenshot. The AI extracts dates, locations, confirmation codes and prices, then files a structured booking into the right trip — and updates your budget and readiness status automatically.
Can my friends plan the trip with me?+
Absolutely. Invite any number of companions into a trip. Everyone works in the same space, uploads bookings, sees changes live — and the group chat includes the AI assistant.
Do people need an account to view my shared trip?+
No. A public share link opens a read-only view of your trip in any browser — no registration, no app install.
What can the Telegram assistant do?+
It sends reminders about trip events, answers questions about your itinerary, shares booking details, locations and files, and gives general travel advice — all inside Telegram, no app needed mid-journey.
Are my documents and data safe?+
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Documents are visible only to you and the companions you invite. Public links show the plan — never your personal documents.
