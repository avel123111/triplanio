Questions, answered.

Everything you might be wondering before your first trip.

Is it really free?
Yes. The core of Triplanio — map, timeline, budget, documents, booking status, your travel archive and public share links — is free forever. Advanced collaboration and the full AI copilot (in-app and in Telegram) are part of Pro. No credit card to start.
How does the AI recognition actually work?
You forward a confirmation — an email, a PDF, plain text, or even a screenshot. The AI reads it and extracts the important parts: dates, confirmation codes and prices. It then creates a proper structured booking that updates your map, timeline, budget and status automatically. You review it; nothing is typed by hand.
Can friends plan with me?
Absolutely. Invite anyone with a single link and plan in one shared space. You'll see a live status of who booked what and what's still open, and the group chat includes the AI as a real member that knows your itinerary. Great for couples and groups alike.
Do viewers need an account to see a shared trip?
No. A public share link opens a beautiful, read-only plan right in the browser — no sign-up and no app install. And private documents are never exposed through a share link.
What can the Telegram assistant do?
It lives in Telegram so there's no app-switching mid-journey. It sends smart reminders (check-ins, departures, cancellation deadlines), answers questions about your trip with bookings, locations and files attached, and offers travel advice on the go.
Is my data safe?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Private documents stay private, and share links never expose personal documents. Your trip is yours.
